Fallback contact card — rendered below the RAG answer when the model couldn't help (or always, depending on meilisearch.rag.fallback.show). Stays invisible when none of email / phone / ticketUrl are set, even if the controller decided to render the partial. Arguments: - fallback {email, phone, ticketUrl, contactName} - question the visitor's current question (pre-filled as the mailto subject so the recipient knows what was asked)
